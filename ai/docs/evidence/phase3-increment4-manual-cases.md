## Observation outside Increment 4 scope (recorded, not fixed)

In case F the matched span started at "short gamma" in an observation and ended at "puts" in the
implication: the `no_trading_recommendation` patterns use `.*` over the whole serialized JSON, so
a directive verb in one field and an instrument noun in another can combine. It rejected correctly
here, but the same mechanism can produce false rejections on legitimate prose such as "dealers are
short gamma" + "the put wall". Fail-closed, so not a safety regression; recorded as finding F-10.
