### MAJOR

- `DataFeed.Application/App/Runtime/ShadowRiskValidator.cs:66` - Missing quote timestamps skip freshness validation; future timestamps also pass. The builder additionally sets `OldestQuoteAt=null` when either leg lacks a timestamp, masking even a known stale opposite leg - Entries can proceed with unverifiable or stale prices during market hours - Reject unknown/future timestamps and validate each leg’s freshness at the decision boundary; replace the test that explicitly expects unknown age to pass.

- `DataFeed.Application/App/Runtime/MetricResolver.cs:132` - VIX Trade validation checks only `Time > 0`, then discards the timestamp - An arbitrarily old VIX print below 30 can pass the gate - Carry timestamp provenance into metric resolution and enforce freshness, returning `NO_DATA` for stale or future trades.

- `DataFeed.Application/App/Runtime/ShadowRuntimeEngine.cs:158` - Position valuation never checks `LegQuote.AsOf` before evaluating exits - Known stale quotes can trigger simulated take-profit/stop-loss closures and record current valuation timestamps - Validate both leg timestamps before valuation/exit decisions; record `VALUATION_NO_DATA` and retain the open position when freshness fails.

- `DataFeed.Application/App/Runtime/MetricResolver.cs:160` - `UsableNetGex` accepts partial coverage whenever any Greeks and any OI exist, ignoring `SymbolsRequested` - Missing put data can leave a positive call-only sum that passes `GEX >= 0`, despite incomplete inputs - Require sufficient, explicitly validated coverage for the intended calculation; add a missing-side regression test.

- `DataFeed.Application/App/Runtime/ShadowRuntimeTick.cs:70` - The switch is captured once before asynchronous metric/candidate requests and never revalidated before storing the fill - Turning OFF while an entry awaits data can still produce a new position afterward - Coordinate OFF with the final entry commit and test switching OFF while candidate construction is pending.
